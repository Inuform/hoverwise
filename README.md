# Hoverwise

Hover over a link to see exactly where it goes.

Hoverwise is a free Chrome extension that shows a small preview card when you hover over a link: the page title, description and preview image, so you can decide whether it's worth opening. It's made for unknown links in stream chats, comments and forums.

**[Get Hoverwise on the Chrome Web Store](STORE_LINK)**

## What it shows

- The page title, description and preview image for any link
- Richer previews for some popular sites, like who posted something, whether a stream is live, or which server an invite leads to
- Where a short link really goes. Short links are followed step by step to their final destination.
- What a file is before you download it, for example "MP4 video · 24.3 MB"

If Hoverwise can't find out what's behind a link, the card says so. It doesn't guess.

## Safety

- Known IP-logging links are never contacted.
- Links to your router or other devices on your home network aren't previewed.
- You get a warning when a link leads straight to a program or archive download, including programs named to look like videos.
- Images from known adult sites and flagged posts are blurred, and the card shows the reason. Press Shift to show the image.

## Settings

Click the Hoverwise icon in Chrome's toolbar to choose:

- **When previews appear:** when you hover a link, only when you press Shift, or off
- **Blur all preview images**

## Privacy

Hoverwise has no accounts, no analytics and no server of its own. To build a preview, it contacts the website the link points to (or that site's own data service), without your cookies. Your settings stay on your device.

Full details: [Privacy policy](https://inuform.github.io/hoverwise/privacy.html)
